6. Algorithm

  1. The initiator records its local process state.
  2. It sends marker messages on its outgoing FIFO channels.
  3. A process receiving its first marker records its local state and sends markers on all its outgoing channels.
  4. It records messages on each incoming channel until that channel's marker is received.
  5. The global snapshot is complete when all local states and channel states have been recorded.

Global State = all recorded process states + all recorded in-transit channel messages.
