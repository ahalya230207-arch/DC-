6. Algorithm Explanation

  1. The initiator records its local state.
  2. It sends a marker on its outgoing channel.
  3. A process receiving its first marker records its local state and sends markers on its outgoing channel.
  4. Each incoming channel is recorded until its marker arrives. Messages received during that interval belong to its channel state.
  5. The snapshot completes after all processes and channels finish recording.

Global State = recorded local process states + recorded in-transit channel messages.
